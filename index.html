03 / SELECTED WORK PROJECT

SUN INVESTMENTS

Capital Markets &
Investment Decision Support

Problem
Sun Investments operates across mutual funds, ETFs, IPOs and portfolio management. The challenge was to determine which instruments and strategies delivered the most attractive risk-adjusted outcomes, where investors were exposed to performance risks, and which market indicators could support more informed advisory decisions.
Solution
A data-driven capital markets assessment framework that compared the performance, risk, liquidity and efficiency of Mutual Funds, IPOs and ETFs — allowing investment decisions to be evaluated beyond headline returns.
