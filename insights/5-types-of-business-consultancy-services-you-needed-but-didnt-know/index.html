03 / INSIGHT / CONSULTING

5 Types of Business Consultancy Services You Needed But Didn't Know

Consulting · Strategy · Operations

Running a business often means knowing a little about everything. There is a difference between solving problems as they appear and building a business that prevents those problems from occurring in the first place.

Running a business often means knowing a little about everything. Firefighting keeps the lights on — but it rarely builds a company that prevents the same problems from returning.

Consultancy is most useful when it fills gaps leaders feel every day but rarely name: strategy that isn’t operational, processes that don’t scale, numbers that arrive too late, or systems that fight the team instead of supporting them.

Five services

What many businesses need — before they ask for it

  • Strategy consulting — clarity on where to play, what to stop, and how growth should happen.
  • Operations consulting — better flow across process, roles, and delivery so scale doesn’t create chaos.
  • Financial & performance consulting — visibility on cost, margin, and the decisions that drive both.
  • Technology & systems consulting — tools and data that match how the business actually works.
  • Organisation & change consulting — structure, habits, and ownership so change lasts.

Sometimes you don’t need to work harder. You need to redesign how the business works.

How to choose

Start with the constraint, not the catalogue.

The right service is the one that unlocks the bottleneck you feel most: unclear direction, messy operations, weak numbers, clunky systems, or change that doesn’t stick. A good partner diagnoses first — then recommends only what moves the business.

The Delphin perspective

We don’t sell a catalogue. We align the whole.

People, processes, and systems only create value when they work together. We help you see the real constraint — then design and implement the change that removes it.
