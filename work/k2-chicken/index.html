The problem

The challenge wasn't
just billing.

The business needed a way to connect everyday transactions with the operational and financial information required to understand what was happening across the business.

  • Sales needed to be recorded
  • Inventory needed visibility
  • Purchases needed tracking
  • Customers needed to be managed
  • Financial information needed to be accessible
  • Business performance needed to be analyzed

The approach

One connected
system.

The POS was developed as more than a billing interface. It connects sales, inventory, purchasing, customers, finance and analysis into one ecosystem.
