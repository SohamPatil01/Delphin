A structured, quantitative framework for evaluating Mutual Funds, IPOs and ETFs — making investment-product research more consistent and easier to translate into advisory insight.

01 / The challenge

Investment product
evaluation.

An investment advisory business was evaluating Mutual Funds, IPOs and ETFs, but the research process involved multiple datasets, performance indicators and analytical approaches.

The opportunity was to create a standardized, quantitative framework that could make investment-product evaluation faster, more consistent and easier to translate into actionable advisory insights.

Return × Risk

We compared investment products across return, volatility and risk-adjusted performance rather than looking at returns alone.

Higher returns do not automatically translate into better investment opportunities.

Equity mutual funds generated higher returns but also carried substantially higher volatility, making risk-adjusted performance an important part of product evaluation.
